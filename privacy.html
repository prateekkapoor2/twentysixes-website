Privacy notice.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Who we are

Twenty Sixes Media is a trading name used for independent creative and content studio activities. For privacy enquiries, contact hello@twentysixes.com.

Information you choose to provide

If you submit the enquiry form, the information you enter is transmitted to Formspree, our form-processing provider, and forwarded to hello@twentysixes.com so we can respond. Please do not include sensitive personal or financial information in the form.

How we use enquiry information

If you email us, we use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry, discuss a potential project and maintain relevant business correspondence. We do not sell personal information.

Third-party services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Enquiry submissions are processed by Formspree under its own privacy terms. If you use our booking link, Calendly processes the scheduling information you provide under its own privacy terms. Your browser may connect directly to these providers when using their services.

Retention and your choices

We retain business correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed for the enquiry, business relationship and applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law.

Changes

We may update this notice when the website or our practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
