Privacy notice.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Who we are

Twenty Sixes Media is a trading name used for independent creative and content studio activities. For privacy enquiries, contact hello@twentysixes.com.

Information you choose to provide

If you submit the enquiry form, the information you provide (such as your name, work email, company, enquiry type and project message) is transmitted to our form-processing provider, Formspree, so that we can receive and respond to your enquiry. The form is not intended for sensitive or confidential information. The form processor may process and retain submissions under its own terms and privacy policy.

How we use enquiry information

We use enquiry information to respond to you, assess and discuss a potential project, arrange meetings and maintain relevant business correspondence. We do not sell personal information.

Third-party services

We use Formspree to process website enquiries and deliver submission notifications to our business email address. We use Calendly to provide appointment scheduling; when you view or use the embedded calendar or booking link, Calendly may process information such as your IP address, browser details and the booking information you submit. Google Fonts may also be loaded from Google servers. Each provider processes information under its own privacy terms. Please review the providers' notices before submitting information through their services.

Retention and your choices

We retain business correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed for the enquiry, business relationship and applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law.

Changes

We may update this notice when the website or our practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
